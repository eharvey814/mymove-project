How It Works

MYMOVE asks three quick questions, then suggests a move that fits your day.

Time

5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes. However much you have.

Feeling

Low energy, stressed, energized, or motivated.

Place

Home, outside, work/school, or the gym.

Your Move in Four Steps

  1. Answer the three questions.
  2. Get a move matched to your answers, or try another.
  3. Follow the steps while the timer counts down.
  4. See your summary and view your moves for the session.
