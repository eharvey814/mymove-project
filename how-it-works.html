How MYMOVE works

MYMOVE asks three quick questions, then suggests a move that fits your day. No plans, no equipment, no pressure.

Step one

Three questions

Pick one answer for each.

Time

How many minutes do you have? Five counts.

  • 5 min
  • 10 min
  • 15 min
  • 30 min

Feeling

Low on energy or wound up? We match your mood.

  • Low energy
  • Stressed
  • Energized
  • Motivated

Place

At your desk, at home, or outside. No gym needed.

  • Home
  • Outside
  • Work/School
  • Gym

Then

Your move in four steps

1

Answer three questions

Tell us your time, how you feel, and where you are.

2

Get a move that fits

We match a move to your answers. Not feeling it? Try another.

3

Follow along

Step-by-step instructions and a timer you can pause or reset.

4

See what you did

Review your time, rate how it felt, and view your moves.

Ready when you are.
