How MYMOVE works

MYMOVE asks three quick questions, then suggests a move that fits your day. No plans, no equipment, no pressure.

Step by step

Your move in four steps

1

Answer three questions

Tell us your time, how you feel, and where you are.

See the questions
How much time do you have?
5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes
How are you feeling?
Low energy, stressed, energized, or motivated
Where are you?
Home, outside, work or school, or the gym
2

Get a move that fits

We match a move to your answers. Not feeling it? Try another.

3

Follow along

An illustrated guide moves with the timer, counting reps and switching sides for you. Pause, rewind, or skip ahead anytime.

4

See what you did

Review your time, rate how it felt, and view your moves.

Ready when you are.
